# Community Workshop Booking

Build a responsive web application for residents to book equipment and workbenches at a nonprofit community workshop. Members should browse resources, see availability in their timezone, reserve or cancel a time slot, and receive a confirmation. Workshop coordinators need to block maintenance periods and review upcoming reservations.

Prevent double bookings and explain conflicts clearly. Collect only the member details needed to manage a reservation. Payments, public marketplace listings, native mobile apps, and access-control hardware integration are not part of the first release.
